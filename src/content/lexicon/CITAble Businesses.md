## The Problem

Most organisations cannot articulate why they exist, how they measure progress, what they are doing about it, or what they have produced. Their strategy lives in slide decks reviewed annually. Their metrics live in dashboards nobody checks. Their projects live in task managers disconnected from strategic intent. Their outputs accumulate without coherent narrative.

The result: organisations that are opaque to themselves and illegible to everyone else. Partners cannot reference their approach. Investors cannot verify their progress. Collaborators cannot build upon their work. The organisation cannot cite its own strategic logic because that logic was never made structurally explicit.

## CITE: The Framework

**CITE™** (Context, Indicators, Tactics, Efforts) structures organisational execution into four bands, each with a distinct function, cadence, and relationship to the others:

| Band | Question Answered | Contains | Change Cadence |
|------|------------------|----------|----------------|
| **C**ontext | What are we, why do we exist, and who do we serve? | Mission, value proposition, positioning, audiences, partnerships, competitive landscape | Quarterly |
| **I**ndicators | How do we know if our strategy is manifesting? | Directional metrics (more of X, less of Y), OKRs, KPIs, pipeline signals, derailment alerts | Weekly/monthly |
| **T**actics | What are we actively doing about it? | Projects, campaigns, initiatives, partnerships in motion. Time-bounded, goal-linked. | Daily |
| **E**fforts | What has this produced? | Publications, proposals, presentations, products, tools. At any progress stage from draft to shipped. | Ongoing |

Each band informs the next. Context defines what Indicators matter. Indicators reveal which Tactics to deploy. Tactics produce Efforts. Efforts feed back into Context: revised positioning, new proof points, updated competitive landscape.

### Efforts vs. Artifacts

The E-band tracks all work at every progress stage: drafts, works-in-progress, internal deliverables, and finished outputs. The subset of completed efforts that ship publicly are **artifacts**: the tangible evidence that the business's strategy is executing. Artifacts are what external observers can point to, reference, and build upon. They are the layer where internal operational process becomes externally visible proof.

This is the bridge from CITE to CITAble: practising CITE consistently produces artifacts with clear lineage (each traceable through tactics, indicators, and context). A business rich in legible artifacts is a CITAble business. Citability is not declared; it is earned through accumulated evidence.

## The Duality: CITE Internally, Become CITAble Externally

The framework operates on two registers simultaneously:

**Internally** ("CITE your business strategy"): the framework provides operational clarity. Every team member, AI agent, or collaborator can navigate from "why do we exist" through "what are we measuring" to "what are we doing" to "what have we shipped." The structure eliminates the coordination overhead that scales with headcount in traditional organisations.

**Externally** ("a CITAble™ business"): when CITE is running well, completed efforts ship as public artifacts with clear strategic lineage. These artifacts are what observers can cite: publications traced to research tactics, products traced to strategic indicators, proposals traced to contextual positioning. The accumulation of legible artifacts is what produces citability. A CITAble business does not claim clarity; it demonstrates it through a body of referenceable work.

The more consistently CITE produces public artifacts with traceable lineage, the more CITAble the business becomes. The process (CITE) produces the quality (CITAble) through sustained practice, not through declaration.

## Time Evolution

Business strategy evolves. Contexts shift as markets move. Indicators reveal unexpected signals. Tactics succeed or fail. Efforts accumulate, stall, or pivot.

CITE is valuable precisely because it tracks this evolution. A living CITE structure creates a documented history of strategic intent and its outcomes: why decisions were made, what the indicators said at the time, which tactics were deployed, and what they produced.

This historical record becomes a key asset for:

- **Knowledge succession**: new team members understand not just what the strategy is, but why it evolved to its current form
- **Stakeholder communication**: investors and partners can trace the logic of strategic shifts
- **Honest retrospection**: the organisation can audit its own decision-making quality over time
- **Scenario planning**: understanding how past context shifts affected all downstream bands informs responses to future shifts

The [Strategy Map](/lexicon/strategy-map) provides a visual tool for exploring this temporal dimension. Where CITE structures what information exists, the Strategy Map renders it as a navigable, time-aware visualization.

## Application to Founder-Led Organisations

CITE is designed for the emerging category of founder-led organisations where small teams outperform large enterprises through AI-augmented operational clarity. The thesis: AI enables companies of tens to match the output of companies of thousands by eliminating bureaucratic and human-resource overhead.

In a Hybrid Intelligence environment, AI agents need structured context to operate effectively. CITE provides that structure:

- Agents read **Context** for strategic grounding before producing work
- Agents monitor **Indicators** for prioritisation signals
- Agents execute within **Tactics** (scoped, time-bounded, goal-linked)
- Agents deposit outputs into **Efforts** (tracked, attributed, progress-visible)

The framework makes operational assumptions explicit, reduces coordination overhead, and enables asynchronous human-AI collaboration at the organisational level. A single founder with a well-structured CITE system and an AI agent fleet achieves the operational coherence that traditional organisations pursue through layers of management.

## Practical Implementation

CITE manifests as a folder architecture within each organisational workspace:

```
{Organisation}/
├── context/           # C — What, why, who (plain folder)
├── indicators/        # I — Metrics and signals (plain folder)
├── tactics/           # T — Active projects and campaigns (plain folder)
├── efforts/           # E — Outputs at any stage (plain folder)
├── _brand/            # Support: brand expression (voice, visuals)
├── _calendar/         # Support: scheduling
├── _journal/          # Support: reflections
└── _legal/            # Support: legal documents
```

The prefix convention signals intended interaction pattern:

- **Plain folders** (context, indicators, tactics, efforts): the CITE bands. Active workspace.
- **Underscore folders** (_brand, _calendar, _journal, _legal): operational support. Inform the bands but sit outside the primary flow.

## Key Properties

- Integrates strategy and execution into a unified, navigable structure
- Produces organisational legibility (internal) and citability (external)
- Tracks time-evolution of strategic intent through documented history
- Designed for AI-augmented, founder-led micro-institutions
- Uses folder architecture as implementation (concrete, not merely conceptual)
- Bridges the gap between "what we believe" (context) and "what we ship" (efforts)
- Scales through AI agents rather than headcount
