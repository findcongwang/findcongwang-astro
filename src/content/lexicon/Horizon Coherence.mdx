Long-term ventures face a critical design challenge. A business cannot build for thirty years toward a single moment where everything clicks together. At any point along its trajectory, the venture must make coherent sense as a viable operation. The years between are not merely preparation for a distant completion; they are the venture's reality.

<RoughAnnotation type="highlight" colour="green">Horizon Coherence is the principle that a venture or portfolio must maintain a coherent, non-void value trajectory across multiple time horizons, with every stage a complete story.</RoughAnnotation>

## The Financial Expression

A long-term net present value is only actualizable by actively building toward it. But at every time stage, the trajectory must hold positive, coherent value. The venture cannot afford voids in its value trajectory unless it has capitalised explicitly to survive them.

| Horizon | Requirement | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Near-term (1-3 years)** | Operational viability and revenue generation | A deep-tech firm commercialising its platform while continuing research |
| **Strategic (5-15 years)** | Sustainable advantage and market position | The same firm achieving market dominance in its niche |
| **Visionary (20-30+ years)** | Transformational impact and enduring legacy | The firm's technology reshaping its entire industry |

## Portfolio Scale Applications

Horizon Coherence becomes most interesting at portfolio scale. A long-term strategic fund can sustain deep-tech or stealth-mode companies precisely because other portfolio companies generate revenue to carry the trajectory through droughts.

**Key insight:** temporal value coherence can be achieved **across the blended portfolio**, not demanded of each company individually. This is a defining property of long-term strategic blended-capital funds.

A single company might require capital to survive a development void, but across the portfolio, revenue-generating ventures sustain the fund's overall trajectory while breakthrough bets incubate.

## Implications for Fund Design

For a fund manager building a long-term strategic portfolio, Horizon Coherence provides critical design guidance:

1. **Drought tolerance design:** The portfolio must contain ventures at different maturity stages, ensuring continuous revenue flow.
2. **Stage sequencing:** Breakthrough bets follow a survivable path, with clear checkpoints ensuring viability at each phase.
3. **Capital structure alignment:** Financing matches the venture's horizon coherence requirements with patient capital.

**The coherent reference frame:** A fund built on Horizon Coherence principles can withstand scrutiny from both financial and mission investors, demonstrating that every company and the portfolio overall maintain viable trajectories at every stage.

## Beyond Venture Investment

The Horizon Coherence principle applies beyond finance:

- **Personal career planning:** Does your professional trajectory hold coherent value across five, fifteen, and thirty-year horizons?
- **Organisational strategy:** Does the company's strategic plan maintain viability across economic cycles and technological disruptions?
- **Community development:** Does an urban regeneration project deliver value at each phase rather than waiting for final completion?

In each case, Horizon Coherence demands that the story makes sense now, not just at some distant finish line.
